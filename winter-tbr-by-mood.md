# Your Winter TBR by Mood

**Heartwarming, steamy, funny, sad, magical or suspenseful: pick the feeling first, then the book.**

[![Holiday Reading & Book Gift Guide 2026](https://lesbianromancereads.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/holiday-books-gift-guide-feature.jpg)](https://lesbianromancereads.com/holiday-books-gift-guide/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=holiday-hub-2026)

The most common way a holiday read goes wrong isn't the book. It's the mood mismatch: a tearjerker when you wanted fluff, an explicit romance when you wanted a closed door, a cozy mystery that turns out to be gruesome. So here are six winter moods, two books each, with the one detail that tells you whether it's right for you tonight.

## ☕ Heartwarming

- **Christmas Mouse** by Rachel Spangler (F/F). Two Christmas superfans bet on whose holiday is better and spend a month hosting each other. Almost pure holiday-tradition comfort.
- **The Christmas Bookshop** by Jenny Colgan. A laid-off shop worker tries to save a dusty Edinburgh bookshop over one festive season. Gentle, closed door.

## 🔥 Steamy

- **A Merry Little Meet Cute** by Julie Murphy & Sierra Simone. Hallmark-movie plot, 5/5 steam on romance.io.
- **Make the Season Bright** by Ashley Herring Blake (F/F). Exes pretend to be strangers at a Colorado family Christmas. 4/5 steam, real yearning.

## 😂 Funny

- **Make You Mine This Christmas** by Lizzie Huxley-Jones (F/F). A mistletoe kiss is mistaken for a relationship; fake dating in the English countryside follows.
- **Hogfather** by Terry Pratchett. Death fills in for the Discworld's Father Christmas. No romance needed.

## 🕯️ Sad, then hopeful

- **Season of Love** by Helena Greer (F/F). An inherited Christmas tree farm, a Jewish family, grief and repair. Closed door.
- **Small Things Like These** by Claire Keegan. 128 pages in a small Irish town before Christmas 1985. Quietly devastating.

## ✨ Magical

- **Good Spirits** by B.K. Borison. The Ghost of Christmas Past falls for the woman he's meant to haunt. Cozy tone, but readers tag it for grief.
- **The Bear and the Nightingale** by Katherine Arden. Medieval Russian winter, household spirits and a frost-demon legend. Not a romance.

## ❄️ Suspenseful

- **The Hunting Party** by Lucy Foley. Friends snowed in at a Highland estate for New Year, and by New Year's Day one of them is dead.
- **Murder at Mistletoe Manor** by F.L. Everett. Twelve strangers snowed into a hotel before Christmas. A cozy whodunit.
